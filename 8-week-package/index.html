What you get

What's in it.

Unlimited classes

Seven coached 50-minute classes a week: HIIT, Barbell & Dumbbell, Functional Fitness and the Saturday Full Body Workout. Come to as many as you can manage. Three or four a week is where most people settle.

Small-group Personal Training

Coached small-group sessions mid-week, mornings 6am to 10am and late afternoons 4pm to 6:50pm, booked around your shifts. This is where I teach the lifts properly, so the classes get safer and harder in the right way.

A nutrition plan I write for you

Not a download. Calorie and protein targets, a meal structure that fits your day, and I adjust it as the weeks go on. Nothing to buy.

The eight weeks

What the package looks like week by week.

Week 1

Weigh in, measure, plan

I weigh you and take a full set of measurements, privately. I write your nutrition plan and book your first classes and Personal Training sessions.

Weeks 2 to 7

Train and adjust

Classes and Personal Training through the week. I check the nutrition and adjust it. Technique gets coached in every session. Nobody drifts off, because I'm expecting you.

Week 8

Measure again, compare

Weighed and measured exactly as in week one. You see the two sets of numbers side by side, and we decide what comes next.

Questions

About the 8-Week Package.

What happens if Stevie is ill?

The terms cover it and they are worth reading before you pay for eight weeks up front. If a class or session is cancelled by Stevie for any reason, illness included, it is moved, and a one-to-one session is rescheduled. If a whole block had to be cancelled and no replacement could be offered, the unused part stays as credit in your name and your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 are not affected. The terms in full.

How many classes can I go to on the 8-Week Package?

All of them. There are seven coached classes a week on the timetable, so up to 56 over the eight weeks, plus the small-group Personal Training sessions mid-week. Most people settle at three or four sessions a week, and that's plenty.

Is the Personal Training one-to-one?

In the 8-Week Package it's coached small-group Personal Training. If you want sessions that are just you and me, that's the one-to-one block, £295 for ten sessions, and plenty of people do that first.

What is in the nutrition plan?

I write it after your week-one consult: your calorie and protein targets, a simple meal structure that fits your work pattern, and adjustments through the eight weeks. Nothing to buy.

What gets measured in week one and week eight?

Body weight and a full set of body measurements, taken the same way both times so the comparison is fair. You get the numbers side by side at the end.

I work shifts. Can I still do it?

Yes. There are 6am classes Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday, Personal Training slots 6am to 10am Tuesday to Friday and 7am and 8am on Saturday, 5:40pm classes Monday to Wednesday, Thursday 5pm and 6pm Personal Training and a Saturday 9am Full Body Workout. Tell me your shift pattern at the consult and we build the week around it.

Is it a contract or direct debit?

No. £205 once, for the eight weeks. It ends when it ends, and you choose whether to go again.

I'm a complete beginner. Will I keep up?

Yes. Every class is scaled on the day, so beginners work alongside experienced people at their own level while I watch technique. The Personal Training sessions are where I teach the basics properly.
