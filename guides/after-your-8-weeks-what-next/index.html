After your 8 weeks: the three options

Finished the 8-Week Package at Sanctuary Fitness? Here are the three honest options for what comes next, and how to choose with your week 8 numbers in front of you.

By Stevie Fulton, head coach at Sanctuary Fitness, Clydebank

Week 8 ends with a tape measure and a decision. Here are the three options, and the honest case for each.

Option one: another eight

The most common choice, for a reason. The first eight weeks build the habit and the technique; the second eight is where the numbers move fastest, because you already know the movements and the room. Same £205, a new set of week 1 measurements, and usually a heavier bar.

Option two: one-to-one Personal Training

For people who found their goal during the package: a specific lift, a race, an injury to work around, or a body-composition target that needs closer attention. Ten private sessions over five weeks, £295, nutrition included.

Option three: a break, on purpose

Sometimes the honest answer is a planned break: a holiday, a house move, a busy month. Take it with a date to come back and the members area still open, and it is a pause rather than a stop. The restart guide is there for when the date arrives.

How to decide

  • If the numbers moved and you enjoyed the room: another eight.
  • If the numbers moved and you now want something specific: one-to-one.
  • If the numbers did not move: the consult, and an honest look at what got in the way. Usually it is one thing, and usually it is fixable.

Whatever you pick, leave a Google review on the way: it is the single most useful thing you can do for the next nervous person, and it takes two minutes at clydebankpt.com/review.
