From Drumchapel Fitclub to Sanctuary Fitness: 2010 to now

The story of Sanctuary Fitness in Clydebank: Drumchapel Fitclub in 2010, the years as DFC Fitness, and the 2026 rebrand to a private coached studio.

By Stevie Fulton, head coach at Sanctuary Fitness, Clydebank

If you searched for DFC Fitness and landed here, you are in the right place. Same coach, same studio, same clients. Here is the short version of how the name changed twice.

2010: Drumchapel Fitclub

Stevie Fulton started coaching group sessions in Drumchapel. No fancy kit, plenty of kettlebells, and word of mouth that filled the sessions within months. The club was built on one idea: busy adults get results when someone coaches them every session.

The DFC Fitness years

Drumchapel Fitclub grew into DFC Fitness and moved to Clydebank. Thousands of people came through the door, the Facebook page passed six thousand followers, and the Google rating settled at 4.9 from clients who kept coming back for package after package.

2026: Sanctuary Fitness

The move to Spectrum House in Clydebank Business Park made it a private studio: coached classes, small-group Personal Training slots and nutrition, all in the 8-Week Package. The name changed to match what it had become. DFC Fitness is a former name on the Google and Facebook listings; the reviews, the coach and the clients carried over.

What has not changed

  • Stevie coaches every session.
  • Programmes are written for you, not downloaded.
  • Results are measured at both ends.
  • No contracts.
