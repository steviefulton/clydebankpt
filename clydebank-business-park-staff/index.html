Questions

Good to know.

Do I need to prove I work on the park?

A work email, a pass or a payslip at the consult is enough. I am not going to interrogate anyone; the price is for the people next door.

Can my employer pay for it?

Yes. I invoice the company and coach the people. Ask me for the letter and hand it to whoever signs off wellbeing spend.

Can a few of us start together?

Yes, and it works well: classes are capped at 25, so message early for the same start date. Colleagues who train together miss fewer sessions.

Is there a lunchtime session?

Personal Training slots run through the day, so a lunch hour works for one-to-one or small-group training. There is no lunchtime class on the timetable at the moment.

What if I leave the company mid-block?

Nothing changes. The block runs to its dates and the price you paid is the price.
