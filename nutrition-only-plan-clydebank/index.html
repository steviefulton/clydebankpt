How it works

Consult, plan, four check-ins, tape.

Four weeks, one plan, one coach who replies.

The consult

Ten minutes, free, on the phone or in the gym: your goal, your week, what you eat now, what has failed before.

The plan

Written within two days of the consult: a calorie target, a protein target, and a week of meals you would actually eat. The recipe bank and protein sheet come with it.

Weeks 1 to 4

Every Sunday you send the numbers and one line about the week. Every Sunday you get an answer and one change, never five.

The end

Measured again. You keep the plan, the numbers and the habits. Carry on for another four, or take it into the 8-Week Package with the classes.

Questions

Good to know.

How much is it?

Message me and I will tell you the price straight out; it is not on this page yet because it is new. No contract, one payment.

Do I have to come to the gym?

Only if you want to be measured here. The consult can be a phone call and the check-ins are WhatsApp, so it works from anywhere in Glasgow or beyond.

Will you sell me supplements or shakes?

No. Nothing is sold here except coaching. Four supplements are worth buying and you can get them anywhere.

Is this the same plan the 8-Week clients get?

Same method and the same rules, written by me. The 8-Week Package adds the coached sessions and the small-group Personal Training slots; this is the food part on its own.

Can I do it while training for a race or a sport?

Yes, and it is one of the best uses of it. Tell me the training week at the consult and the plan is built round it, including the hard days.
