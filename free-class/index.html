Questions people ask about the free class

Is the class free?

Yes. One coached class, nothing to pay and nothing to sign. You are not joining anything by taking it and you can say thanks and go at the end.

Who can use the voucher?

Anybody new to the gym. One class per person, at Wednesday the 30th of September, 5:40pm or Saturday the 3rd of October, 9am. If you already train here, pass it to somebody who does not.

How do I book the place?

Scan the code on the voucher, or tap the WhatsApp button on this page, and the message is already written. Say which of the two dates suits and press send. If you would rather email, sanctuary@clydebankpt.com reaches the same person.

What if the class is full?

Classes are capped at 25 people, so the places go by the order they are asked for. If the date you wanted is full I will say so and offer you the other one.

Do I need to be fit to come?

No. Every movement is shown, every weight is optional, and the class is scaled to whoever is in it that day. Plenty of people start having done nothing for years.

What do I bring?

Trainers, something you can move in and a bottle of water. Come ten minutes early and I will show you round before it starts.

What happens after the free class?

Nothing you have not asked for. If you want to carry on, the next 8-Week Package starts Monday the 28th of September at £205, or £185 if you work on Clydebank Business Park. There is no contract.
