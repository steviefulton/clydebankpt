Questions

Things people ask before they message.

Thirty straight answers. If yours is not here, WhatsApp Stevie and it will be.

Starting

How do I start?

Message Stevie on WhatsApp for a free consult, or book a free consult. No forms, no card, no contract.

Do I need to be fit already?

No. Every class is scaled to the person doing it. If you want to build up first, start with a Personal Training slot.

Can I try before I pay?

The free consult comes first, always, and if a class is the right way to start I will book you into one before you pay for anything. No card, no contract.

When does the next 8-Week Package start?

Monday Monday 28 September. Classes are capped at 25 people, so places are limited, so message early.

Prices

How much is the 8-Week Package?

£205, one payment. Every class on the timetable, small-group Personal Training slots, a nutrition plan, and measurements in week 1 and week 8.

How much is one-to-one PT?

£295 for ten private sessions, two a week over five weeks, nutrition included. Kids welcome at your session.

Is there a contract or a joining fee?

No. One payment, a start and a finish. You decide what comes next.

Can I pay in instalments?

Ask me at the consult. Deposits and split payments are sorted person to person.

Do you do gift vouchers?

Yes. Message me and I will sort a voucher for the 8-Week Package or one-to-one sessions.

Classes and times

What classes are there?

HIIT, Barbell & Dumbbell, Functional Fitness and the Saturday Full Body Workout. Sixty minutes, technique coached in every session.

What time are classes?

6am classes Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday; 5:40pm classes Monday to Wednesday; Saturday 9am Full Body Workout. Personal Training slots 7am to 10am Tuesday to Friday (from 6am on Thursday) and 7am and 8am on Saturday, plus afternoons: 4pm Tuesday, 4pm Wednesday, 5pm and 6pm Thursday.

How big are the classes?

Small enough that Stevie sees every session. That is why places are limited.

Do I need to book classes?

Yes, through the Bookfit app once you are a client. Stevie sets you up at the consult.

What if I miss a session?

Book another one. A missed session is a missed session, not a missed week.

Can I do classes and PT?

The 8-Week Package includes both.

The studio

Where are you?

Spectrum House, 1A North Avenue, Clydebank Business Park, G81 2DR. Beside Singer station, free parking outside.

Is it a normal gym?

No. It is a private coached studio. No membership, no wandering about wondering what to do. You come in for a session and Stevie runs it.

Is there parking?

Yes, free, right outside the building.

Are there changing rooms and showers?

Changing space and toilets, yes. Ask Stevie about showers when you book.

Is it step-free?

Ask me before your first visit and I will tell you exactly what the access is like for you.

Results and nutrition

What results can I expect?

It depends on what you do. Most 8-Week clients who train three times a week and follow the plan lose fat, get noticeably stronger and feel a lot better. Your week 1 and week 8 numbers tell the truth.

Do you do nutrition?

Yes. Every package includes a nutrition plan written for you, and the members area has the Sanctuary baseline guide and a calorie calculator.

Do I have to give up alcohol or takeaways?

No. They get counted, not banned.

I have an injury or a health condition. Can I still train?

Usually yes, with a programme built around it. Tell me at the consult, and check with your GP if you are unsure.

Are you insured and qualified?

Yes. Stevie has been coaching since 2010, is fully qualified and insured.

Who it is for

I am over 50. Is this for me?

Yes. A good share of the room is over 40, and strength work matters more the older you get.

I am pregnant or just had a baby.

Talk to Stevie and your midwife or GP first. Postnatal training is done one-to-one with a programme that respects where you are.

Can I bring my kids?

To one-to-one Personal Training sessions, yes.

Do you do sports-specific training?

Yes, one-to-one. Football, running, boxing, golf: strength work built around your sport.

Do you do corporate sessions?

Yes, for Clydebank Business Park companies and anyone nearby. Message me with numbers and times.

People also ask

Is Sanctuary Fitness a gym or a personal trainer?

Both, in the sense that matters: a private coached studio with racks, bars and floor space, where every session is coached by Stevie. It is not a 24-hour gym you wander round on your own.

How much does a personal trainer cost in Clydebank?

Here, one-to-one Personal Training is £295 for ten sessions over five weeks with nutrition included. The 8-Week Package with classes and PT slots is £205. There is no joining fee.

Are there fitness classes in Clydebank for beginners?

Yes. HIIT, Barbell and Dumbbell, Functional Fitness and the Saturday Full Body Workout are all-levels classes, scaled per person, and the first week is free.

Is there a 6am class in Clydebank?

Yes. Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday at 6:00am, coached, done by 7 so you are at work on time.

What is the best way to lose weight in 8 weeks?

Coached strength training two or three times a week, a protein-first nutrition plan with a calorie target you can keep, more steps than you do now, and a measurement at the start and the end. That is the 8-Week Package.

How many times a week should I train?

Three coached sessions a week gets most people the result they came for. Two is fine to start. Six is available if you want it.

How much protein should I eat to lose fat?

Roughly 1.6 to 2 grams per kilo of bodyweight a day for most people training hard. The free calculator on the site gives you a number in thirty seconds.

Can I do personal training with an injury?

Usually, yes, around it rather than through it. Tell me at the consult, bring the advice from your physio, and the session is written for what you can do today.

Is personal training worth it over 40?

It is where it pays off most. Muscle drops faster after 40 unless you lift, and coached lifting is the safest way to start. A good chunk of the room is 40 to 60.

Do you do couples personal training?

Yes. Two people, one coach, one session, both measured. Message Stevie for the two-person price.

Can I train while on Mounjaro or Wegovy?

That is a question for your prescriber first. If they are happy, coached strength work and protein-first eating are exactly what protects your muscle while the weight comes off.

Do you offer menopause fitness classes?

Strength training and conditioning built around recovery, in mixed all-levels classes or one-to-one. The menopause page explains what changes and what helps.

Is there parking at the studio?

Free parking at Spectrum House, inside Clydebank Business Park.

How far is the studio from Singer station?

About five minutes on foot.

Do you take walk-ins?

Message first. Classes are small and coached, so Stevie needs to know you are coming. A WhatsApp an hour before is usually fine.

Can I bring my kids?

To one-to-one Personal Training sessions, yes. Not to classes.

Do you sell supplements or meal plans?

No supplements. The nutrition plan is included in every package, and the recipe bank and protein cheat sheet are free on the site.

What should I wear to my first session?

Trainers, something you can squat in, a water bottle. Nothing else needed.

Do you offer online or remote coaching?

Not as a product. Everything here is in the room, coached in person. Members get the plan, the recipes and the tracker online between sessions.

How do I leave a Google review?

clydebankpt.com/review takes you straight to the form. Two minutes, and it helps the next nervous person more than anything.
