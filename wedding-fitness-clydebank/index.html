Questions

Good to know.

How far before the wedding should I start?

Ten weeks is ideal: eight for the package and a quiet fortnight before the day. If you have less, message me anyway; a shorter run is still worth doing, I just will not pretend it does the same job.

Will I lose weight in time?

Most people lose fat and inches over eight coached weeks with the nutrition plan. What I will not do is promise a number. You get honest coaching, measurements at both ends and a plan you can actually follow through the run-up.

Can my partner or the bridal party come too?

Yes. Everyone trains in the one block, coached at their own level, and the people who start together tend to keep each other turning up.

What about the stag or hen weekend?

Go and enjoy it. Tell me the dates and the plan bends around them; the holiday guide covers exactly what to do that weekend and the week after.
