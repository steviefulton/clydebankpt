    What the answer means

    There are two ways in and the quiz picks between them on how you answered about confidence, time and what you want out of it. The 8-Week Package is £205: every coached class on the timetable, the small-group Personal Training slots, a nutrition plan written for you, and the same measurements taken in week one and week eight so the eight weeks can be judged on something other than a feeling. One-to-one Personal Training is £295 for ten private sessions over five weeks, nutrition included, with nobody else in the session.

    What to do with it: the answer is a recommendation, not a decision. Plenty of people who get "one-to-one" start with the package because they want the other folk beside them, and plenty who get "8-Week Package" do a one-to-one block first to learn the lifts. Send the answer to Stevie and the free ten-minute consult starts from it instead of from nothing. Nothing is paid for at that point and nothing is signed.
