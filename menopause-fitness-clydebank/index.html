What changes

Why the old approach stops working.

More cardio and less food is exactly the wrong prescription after 40.

Muscle drops faster

So we build it on purpose: barbells, dumbbells, progressive loading, twice a week minimum.

Recovery takes longer

So sessions are planned around it. Hard days, easy days, sleep as a training variable.

Weight shifts to the middle

So nutrition is protein and structure first, not starvation. The tape measure tells the truth the scales hide.

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need a GP letter?

Not usually. If you are on HRT or have a condition, tell Stevie at the consult and check with your GP if you are unsure.

I have never lifted weights.

Nearly nobody has when they start. Technique is coached from empty bars and light dumbbells.

Is it a women-only class?

No. Classes are mixed, all levels, and one-to-one Personal Training is always an option.
