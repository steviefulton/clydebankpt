Questions

Good to know.

Am I too old to start lifting?

No. Strength training is the single best thing to start in your 40s, 50s and beyond, and it is coached here from the ground up.

What about my bad back or knees?

Tell me at the consult. I write the programme around what you can do and progress it from there. If something needs a physio first, I'll say so.

Are the classes full of twenty-year-olds?

No. The room is mostly 30 to 60, working people, all levels.
