Questions

Good to know.

Is nutrition coaching extra?

No. It is included in both the 8-Week Package and one-to-one PT.

Do I have to track everything I eat?

Not forever. Most people track for the first couple of weeks to learn portions, then run on the structure.

Can you help with medical diets?

I'll work around common needs, and I'll tell you honestly if something needs a dietitian or GP first.
