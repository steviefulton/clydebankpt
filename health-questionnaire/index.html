Before your first session

Two minutes of health questions.

The standard pre-exercise questionnaire every new client answers before the first session. Nothing is saved on this website: your answers become a message that you send to Stevie yourself, on WhatsApp or by email.

Health

Answer honestly. A "Yes" does not stop you training; it tells Stevie what to plan around, and sometimes means a quick word with your GP first.

1. Has a doctor ever said you have a heart condition, or that you should only do physical activity recommended by a doctor?
2. Do you feel pain in your chest when you do physical activity?
3. In the past month, have you had chest pain when you were not doing physical activity?
4. Do you lose your balance because of dizziness, or do you ever lose consciousness?
5. Do you have a bone or joint problem (for example back, knee or hip) that could be made worse by a change in your physical activity?
6. Is a doctor currently prescribing medication for blood pressure, a heart condition, diabetes, or anything else we should know about?
7. Are you pregnant, or have you given birth in the last six months?
8. Do you know of any other reason why you should not do physical activity?

One-to-one Personal Training. The one-to-one block is ten private sessions, usually two a week over five weeks, and all ten must be used within ten weeks of the first session unless we agree otherwise in writing before the block starts. Cancel a class in the app if you cannot make it. Turn up in time for the warm-up; for your own safety I may not let you join a class more than ten minutes after it has started.

If I have to cancel. If a class or session is cancelled by me (illness, weather, the building, an emergency) it is moved or, for one-to-one, rescheduled.
