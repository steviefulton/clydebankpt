Terms · Updated 4 September 2026

The terms, in plain English.

In one line: one payment, no contract; the £68.50 deposit comes off the balance.

Fifteen short rules so nobody is surprised later. They are written to be fair to the person who turns up and to the person who paid for the place beside them. Anything not covered here: message me and we will sort it like adults.

  1. Home
  2. Terms

1. Payment

The 8-Week Package (£205) and the one-to-one block (£295) are paid in full before the first session, by bank transfer or by card in the gym. A place is confirmed when it is paid. There is no contract, no direct debit and no notice period.

2. Holding a place

A deposit of £68.50, a third of the 8-Week Package, holds a place on the next start date. It comes off the balance, which is due before the first session. The deposit is not refunded in cash. If plans change it moves to the next start, or stays as credit for a later package. Bank transfer or card in the gym; message me and I send the details.

3. Late cancellation, one-to-one

A one-to-one session cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice counts as used. The first late cancellation in a block is let go with a reminder; after that they count.

4. Missed classes

Classes are unlimited within the package, so a missed class costs nothing. If a class is full and someone who booked does not turn up twice in a row, they book the following week on the day rather than in advance, so the place goes to someone who will use it.

5. Refunds and credit

There are no cash refunds. If you stop before or during a block, what you have paid stays as credit and can be used against a later package or block.

6. Health

Everyone completes the health questionnaire before the first session and tells me about any change. If it flags something, I will ask you to check with your GP before we start. I can stop a session or pause a block if I think training is unsafe; we sort what happens next person to person.

7. Children

Children are welcome at one-to-one sessions only, stay in the spot we agree, and remain the parent's responsibility at all times. No children in group classes. How that works.

8. Photos and names

No client is photographed, filmed or named without written consent on the gym consent form. Consent can be withdrawn at any time and the photo or video comes down the day you ask.

9. Gift vouchers

Valid for twelve months from purchase, for the block named on the voucher, one voucher per person, not exchangeable for cash. Gift vouchers.

10. Price changes

A price change never affects a block that has already been paid for.

11. Behaviour

I can refuse or end a block, with the unused part kept as credit, for anyone who is abusive to me or to other clients. It has not happened yet.

12. Your data

The site uses no cookies. WhatsApp messages and the health questionnaire are kept only while you are a client plus one year, and deleted on request. The privacy notice has the detail.

13. Changes to these terms

This page is dated. If a term changes, the date changes and the new term applies from the next block, never to one already paid for.
