Terms · Updated 4 September 2026

The terms, in plain English.

Fifteen short rules so nobody is surprised later. They are written to be fair to the person who turns up and to the person who paid for the place beside them. Anything not covered here: message me and we will sort it like adults.

1. Payment

The 8-Week Package (£205) and the one-to-one block (£295) are paid in full before the first session, by bank transfer or by card in the studio. A place is confirmed when it is paid. There is no contract, no direct debit and no notice period.

2. Holding a place

A deposit of £68.50, a third of the 8-Week Package, holds a place on the next start date. It comes off the balance, which is due before the first session. The deposit is refunded in full if you cancel seven or more days before the start; inside seven days it moves to the next start instead of being refunded. Bank transfer or card in the studio; message me and I send the details.

3. Late cancellation, one-to-one

A one-to-one session cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice counts as used. The first late cancellation in a block is let go with a reminder; after that they count.

4. Missed classes

Classes are unlimited within the package, so a missed class costs nothing. If a class is full and someone who booked does not turn up twice in a row, they book the following week on the day rather than in advance, so the place goes to someone who will use it.

5. Freezing a block

One freeze per 8-Week Package, of up to two weeks, for illness, injury or a holiday told to me in advance. The block extends by the same length. No freezes in the final week.

6. Transferring a block

An unused block can be transferred to another person once, before it starts, at no cost. Once it has started it stays with the person who bought it.

7. Refunds

Full refund up to 48 hours before the first session. After the first session there is no refund, but the freeze and transfer rules above apply. Refunds go back the way they were paid, within 14 days.

8. Health

Everyone completes the health questionnaire before the first session and tells me about any change. If it flags something, I will ask you to check with your GP before we start. I can stop a session or pause a block if I think training is unsafe; the freeze rule then applies.

9. Children

Children are welcome at one-to-one sessions only, stay in the spot we agree, and remain the parent's responsibility at all times. No children in group classes. How that works.

10. Photos and names

No client is photographed, filmed or named without written consent on the studio consent form. Consent can be withdrawn at any time and the photo or video comes down the day you ask.

11. Gift vouchers

Valid for twelve months from purchase, for the block named on the voucher, one voucher per person, not exchangeable for cash. Gift vouchers.

12. Price changes

A price change never affects a block that has already been paid for.

13. Behaviour

I can refuse or end a block, with a pro-rata refund, for anyone who is abusive to me or to other clients. It has not happened yet.

14. Your data

The site uses no cookies. WhatsApp messages and the health questionnaire are kept only while you are a client plus one year, and deleted on request. The privacy notice has the detail.

15. Changes to these terms

This page is dated. If a term changes, the date changes and the new term applies from the next block, never to one already paid for.
