Real results

The week one and week eight photos.

The people you see here are real Sanctuary Fitness clients. I took the week one and week eight photos myself, the same way both times, and every one of them is here because that client gave written consent on the health questionnaire. Consent can be withdrawn by message and the photo comes down within a day of anyone asking. Everyone starts from a different place, so results vary, but these are genuine examples of what people have achieved through consistent training, better eating and sticking with the process. No magic transformations, just people putting the work in.

How the photos are taken

Same spot in the gym, same light, same distance, same time of day, week one and week eight. I take them so they compare fairly; a phone selfie in different light can make anyone look better or worse. The client keeps the photos. They only appear here if the client chooses to share them, and they come down the day they ask.

What the numbers behind them look like

Weighed and measured with a tape at both ends, privately. Most people lose inches before the scales move much, which is why the tape is the measure I trust. What eight weeks of fat loss really looks like goes through the typical shape of it, and why measurements beat the scales explains the tape.

What made the difference

  • Three or four coached sessions a week, every week, for eight weeks.
  • A nutrition plan written for them, protein first, nothing to buy.
  • Walking every day.
  • Turning up on the weeks they did not fancy it.
