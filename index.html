Who this is for

Busy adults who want results, not a gym membership they never use.

Most people who come to Sanctuary Fitness aren't athletes or fitness models. They're busy people who want to lose weight, get stronger, feel healthier and finally stick to something. Some haven't trained in years, some are complete beginners, and plenty have paid for a big gym every month without actually going. Here you're coached, supported and kept accountable from the day you start.

Busy and stuck

You know roughly what you should be doing, you just never manage to do it consistently. That's what the structure is for: classes at set times, Personal Training sessions booked in, and a nutrition plan written for you, so there's nothing to work out on your own.

New or nervous

Never lifted a barbell, or not since school? Nearly everyone starts there. I'll teach you the movements properly, at a pace that suits you, and nobody in the room is watching to judge.

Getting back to it

Coming back after an injury, a bad back, a baby or a long spell off? We'll build you up sensibly, without flare-ups, and you'll be surprised how quickly it comes back.

The honest comparison

Why Sanctuary Fitness instead of a big gym?

There's nothing wrong with a big gym, but for most people access to equipment isn't the problem. The problem is knowing what to do, staying consistent and having someone there to push you when motivation drops. Here you don't just turn up and hope for the best: your training is coached, your technique is checked, your progress is measured, and someone notices if you've disappeared for two weeks. That's what helps people actually stick with it.

Meet your coach

Stevie Fulton.

I'm Stevie Fulton and I've been coaching people across Glasgow and West Dunbartonshire since 2010. It started as Drumchapel Fitclub, grew into DFC Fitness, and is now Sanctuary Fitness here in Clydebank. Over the years I've worked with complete beginners, busy parents, shift workers and people who just wanted to get back into shape after a long break. My approach is simple: train properly, keep it realistic and build habits you can actually keep. You don't need to be fit before you start and you don't need to know how to lift. You just need to turn up and put the work in, and I'll help with the rest.

  • Level 3 Personal Trainer (CIMSPA recognised), with an HND in Health, Fitness & Exercise from Glasgow Clyde College
  • Level 4 Sports Nutrition (ORIGYM) and Level 4 Obesity & Weight Management (FIE)
  • Strength & Conditioning diploma (UKSCA) and Level 3 Bootcamp (YMCA Awards)
  • Coaching since 2010, with five years in the British Army before that
  • Fat loss, strength for over-30s, and getting people back to training after time off

Questions

Things people ask before they book.

I've never trained before. Is that a problem?

No. Most people who start here are beginners or coming back after years off. Every class is scaled to you on the day, and the Personal Training slots teach the movements from scratch. More for beginners.

What does the 8-Week Package include?

Every group class on the timetable (seven a week), coached small-group Personal Training sessions mid-week (mornings 6am to 10am and late afternoons 4pm to 7pm), a tailored nutrition plan, and a weigh-in and measurements in week one and again in week eight so you can see exactly what changed. It's £205 one-off. One-to-one PT isn't part of it; that's the separate £295 block.

How much is a personal trainer in Clydebank?

Here, one-to-one personal training is £295 for ten sessions over five weeks (two a week), with nutrition and a programme written for you, which works out at £29.50 a session. The 8-Week Package with classes and small-group PT is £205. See prices.

Are there 6am classes?

Yes. 6am HIIT on Tuesday, 6am Barbell & Dumbbell on Wednesday, 6am Personal Training on Thursday, 6am Functional Fitness on Friday, plus Personal Training from 6am. Timetable.

Is this the same place as DFC Fitness?

Yes. DFC Fitness became Sanctuary Fitness in August 2026. Still at Spectrum House and still coached by me; only the name changed. The story.

Where exactly are you?

Spectrum House, 1A North Avenue, Clydebank Business Park, Clydebank G81 2DR. It's a short walk from Singer station and there's parking at the building. Directions.

Can I bring my kids to a session?

Yes, to one-to-one sessions. Plenty of parents train with their children in the studio.

Do I need to sign a contract?

No. The 8-Week Package and the five-week one-to-one block are one-off payments with a clear start and finish.

Do you do nutrition plans?

Yes. A tailored plan is included in both packages, written around your shifts and your kitchen. Nutrition coaching.
