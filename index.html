Who this is for

Busy adults who want results, not a gym membership they never use.

People come from Clydebank and all over Glasgow. Most are working long hours or shifts, have joined a big gym before and drifted off, and want someone to actually coach them. That is exactly what Sanctuary Fitness is.

Busy and stuck

You know what you should be doing but never do it consistently. Classes are at set times, Personal Training is booked in, and the nutrition plan is written for you.

New or nervous

Never lifted a barbell, or not since school. Every movement is coached from scratch in a private studio, not a crowded gym floor.

Getting back to it

Coming back from an injury, a bad back, a baby, or a long time off. Sensible progressions that build you back up without flare-ups.

Questions

Things people ask before they book.

I have never trained before. Is that a problem?

No. Most people who start at Sanctuary Fitness are beginners or coming back after years off. Every class is coached and scaled to you on the day, and Personal Training sessions teach the movements from scratch.

What does the 8-Week Package include?

Every group class on the timetable (seven a week), Personal Training sessions mid-week between 6am and 7pm, a tailored nutrition plan, a full weigh-in and measurements in week one, and the same again in week eight so you can see exactly what changed. It costs £205.

How much is a personal trainer in Clydebank?

At Sanctuary Fitness, one-to-one personal training is £295 for ten sessions over five weeks (two a week), including nutrition and a programme written for you. That works out at £29.50 a session.

Is this the same place as DFC Fitness?

Yes. DFC Fitness became Sanctuary Fitness in August 2026. Still at Spectrum House, still coached by Stevie Fulton. Only the name changed.

Where exactly are you?

Spectrum House, 1A North Avenue, in Clydebank Business Park, G81 2DR. It is a short walk from Singer station and there is parking at the building.

Can I bring my kids to a session?

Yes, to one-to-one PT sessions. Plenty of parents train with their children in the studio.

Do I need to sign a contract?

No. The 8-Week Package and the 5-week one-to-one block are one-off payments with a clear start and finish.
