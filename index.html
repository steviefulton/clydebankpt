Who this is for

Who trains here.

Normal people. Mums and dads, shift workers, folk who sit at a desk all day. Some have never trained, some used to and stopped, and most have paid for a gym they never went to. They come here because someone coaches them, the sessions run at set times, and they are measured at the start and the end. So it actually happens.

Busy and stuck

You know what you should be doing. You just don't do it. Set class times, Personal Training slots booked in, and a food plan written for you take the thinking out of it.

New or nervous

Never lifted a bar, or not since school? Most people start there. I teach you the movements properly, at your pace, and nobody in the gym is judging.

Getting back to it

After an injury, a bad back, a baby or a long spell off. I build you up sensibly, and it comes back quicker than you think.
