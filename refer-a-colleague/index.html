Why this is a different conversation

Talking someone at your work into a gym is not the same as talking a pal into one. The reason a colleague has not started is almost never the money and almost always the hour: the drive, the parking, getting back for a meeting. None of that applies here, because the gym is in the same business park as their desk. Spectrum House, 1A North Avenue. They can walk to it in the time it takes to find a space at a town-centre gym.

So the thing to send them is not a motivational line. It is the address, the time, and the price they pay.

What they actually get

  • The 8-Week Package at £185 instead of £205, one payment, no contract and no membership underneath it.
  • Every coached class on the timetable: 6am classes Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday, 5:40pm classes Monday to Wednesday and Saturday 9am Full Body Workout, 50 minutes each, never more than 25 in a class.
  • The small-group Personal Training slots, which is where a lunch hour fits if the class times do not.
  • A nutrition plan written for them at the consult, and the tape measure in week one and week eight so the result is a number.
  • A free ten-minute consult first, in the gym or on the phone.

Three ways to pass it on

  • Send their name. One message with a first name and which class they fancy, and Stevie takes it from there.
  • Send the PDF. One page, built for forwarding: the price, the times, the address and nothing to sign.
  • Send it up. If your office manager or HR are looking for something for staff, the company can buy places and be invoiced. The staff price page has the detail, and the corporate page has the formats.

What happens when they message

  1. Free consult. Ten minutes. What they want, what has got in the way before, anything sore. No card.
  2. The park price applied. Work email, pass or payslip at the consult and it is £185. One place per person per block.
  3. They start on the Monday. The next block starts Monday the 28th of September.

Colleagues who start together tend to finish together, which is the only honest argument for doing this at all: the person who has someone at the next desk asking how it went on Tuesday turns up on Thursday.

Questions

Do they have to work for my company?

No. The £185 price is for anyone employed by a company based on Clydebank Business Park, contractors on site included. Different building, different firm, same park, same price.

What do they have to show?

A work email, a pass or a payslip at the free consult. That is the whole check.

Can the company pay for a few of us at once?

Yes. Stevie invoices the company and coaches the people; every place is at the park price whether the firm pays or the person does. How a company block works.

Is there a lunchtime class we could all do?

No. There is no lunchtime class on the timetable. The Personal Training slots run through the day, so a lunch hour works for one-to-one or small-group training, and the classes are at 6am and 5:40pm.

What if they are not sure yet?

Send them the free ten-minute consult rather than the price. Gym or phone, no card, no contract, and they can come and watch a class before they decide.

Is this the same as bringing a mate?

It is the same gym and a different conversation. Bringing a mate is for friends and family anywhere; this one is for the people you work beside, where the price and the walk are the argument.
