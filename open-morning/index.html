Why there is an open morning at all

Most people who are thinking about a gym are not weighing up the price. They are wondering what it looks like inside and who else is in there, and there is no honest way to answer that in writing. So the Saturday before every block starts, the door stays open for an hour and a bit and anyone can come and look.

There is no talk, no group, no sign-up sheet. Stevie is there, the gym is there, and you can ask whatever you were going to ask by message, out loud, in the gym it is about.

When and where

  • Saturday the 26th of September, 10:15am to 11:30am. Come at any point in that hour and a quarter.
  • Spectrum House, 1A North Avenue, Clydebank Business Park, Clydebank G81 2DR. The front door is what3words ///pretty.needed.chill.
  • Free parking right outside the building. No meter, no app, no time limit.
  • Five minutes' walk from Singer station, ten from the Clyde Shopping Centre.
  • If you cannot find the door, message 07376 941421 and Stevie will come out for you.

What is actually happening while you are there

The 9am Full Body Workout finishes at 9:50am, so the floor is clear. The measurement corner is set up, the results board is out, and Stevie is doing the free ten-minute consult on the spot for anyone who wants one. That is the same ten minutes anybody gets by message: what you want to change, what has got in the way before, anything sore, and which of the two options fits or whether neither does.

You can also just have a coffee, look at the kit and go. Nobody will follow you to the car.

What it costs

Nothing. There is no card, no contract and no form. The two prices are on the prices page and they do not change because you turned up in person.

The block it sits two days before

The open morning is the Saturday before a start, and this one is two days before the next 8-Week Package.

Next 8-Week Package starts Monday the 28th of September. 15 days to go. 3 places left. £205, free consult first.

If you already know you want in, the thing to read is what the first Monday looks like. If you work on the estate, the price is different: £185 for anyone employed on Clydebank Business Park.

Say you are coming

One message with your first name is enough. Email instead if that is easier.

Questions

Do I have to book?

No. The door is open from 10:15am and you can walk in. A message first is helpful, because it means Stevie knows to look out for you and there is enough coffee, but nobody is turned away for not sending one.

What do I need to bring?

Nothing. You are not training. Come in what you are wearing.

Will I be sold something?

No. There are two prices on the site, £205 and £295, and they are the same two prices in the gym. If you want the ten-minute consult while you are there you can have it; if you want to look at the place and leave, that is what the morning is for.

Can I bring the kids?

Yes. Nobody is training, so there is no reason they cannot come.

Is there a class on before it?

Yes. The 9am Full Body Workout finishes at 9:50am, which is why the morning starts at 10:15am: the floor is clear by then.

What if I cannot make that Saturday?

Message Stevie and have the ten-minute consult on the phone or at the gym on a day that suits. The open morning is a convenience, not the only way in.
