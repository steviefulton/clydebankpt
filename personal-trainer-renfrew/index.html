Is Sanctuary Fitness far from Renfrew?

About fifteen minutes. Fifteen minutes via the Clyde Tunnel and the Clydeside Expressway, or over the Erskine Bridge and along the A814. Free parking at the building.

What does it cost?

The 8-Week Package is £205: unlimited classes, small-group Personal Training, a nutrition plan and measurements at both ends. One-to-one PT is £295 for ten sessions over five weeks.

Yes. Most people who start with me are beginners or coming back after years off. Every session is coached and scaled to you.
