Questions

About one-to-one PT.

How much is one-to-one personal training in Clydebank?

At Sanctuary Fitness it is £295 for ten one-to-one sessions over five weeks, two a week, including a tailored programme and nutrition plan. That is £29.50 a session.

When can I train?

One-to-one sessions run mid-week, mornings 6am to 10am and late afternoons 4pm to 7pm, and are booked directly with Stevie around your work.

Can I bring my children?

Yes. Kids can come along to one-to-one PT sessions. Plenty of parents train this way.

What if I have an injury or a bad back?

Tell me at the consult. I write the programme around what you can do today and build it up from there. If something needs a physio first, I'll say so.

What happens after the five weeks?

You decide. Many people move onto the 8-Week Package once they know the movements, some book another block, some are happy to train on their own with the programme they now have.

Is one-to-one or the 8-Week Package better for me?

One-to-one if you want full attention, have an injury to work around, or need sessions to fit a tight schedule. The 8-Week Package if you like training with others and want the most sessions for the money. The free consult sorts this out.
